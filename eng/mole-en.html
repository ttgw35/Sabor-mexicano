Mole Fair

Every October, Puebla dresses in aromas and flavors with the Mole Fair. An event that celebrates one of Mexico's most emblematic dishes: mole poblano. Artisans, cooks and visitors gather to taste varieties of mole, participate in workshops and enjoy cultural presentations.

It is an experience that unites tradition, family and the country's culinary richness.
