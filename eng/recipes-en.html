Recipes

Beef birria is a traditional Mexican stew originating from Jalisco. It is characterized by beef cooked slowly until tender and juicy, bathed in a marinade of dried chilies, spices and aromatic herbs that give it a deep flavor.

Burritos are a preparation originating from northern Mexico, made with flour tortillas filled with meat, beans and cheese. They are practical, delicious and very popular both in Mexico and the United States.

Ranch steak is a typical dish of Mexican home cooking. It is prepared with beef steak stewed in tomato sauce with onion, garlic and chilies. It is ideal to accompany with rice and refried beans.
