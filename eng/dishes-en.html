Dishes by Region

Tacos al Pastor

Originating from Mexico City, tacos al pastor combine meat marinated in achiote, pineapple and onion, slowly cooked on a vertical spit. This dish was born from the Arabic influence of shawarma, adapted with the unique touch of Mexican flavor.

Pozole

Pozole is a festive soup made with cacahuazintle corn, pork or chicken, and accompanied with lettuce, radishes, oregano and lime. Each region has its version, but all share the spirit of celebration that accompanies this dish.

Tamales

Tamales are an ancestral legacy of pre-Hispanic cuisine. Prepared with corn dough and filled with stews, wrapped in corn or banana leaves, they are enjoyed throughout the country during celebrations and family festivities.

Enchiladas

Enchiladas represent the perfect balance between spicy and traditional. They are prepared with tortillas dipped in red or green sauce, filled with chicken or cheese, and topped with cream, onion and fresh cheese. A classic that varies from state to state.
