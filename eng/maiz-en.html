Corn Fair

In the State of Mexico, every December the Corn Fair is celebrated, paying tribute to the most important ingredient in Mexican gastronomy. It features tamales, esquites, elotes and a multitude of dishes that have corn as the protagonist.

A fair full of flavor, culture and tradition that keeps the heritage of the Mexican countryside alive.
